While working on godoom I wanted to add clouds and sun to the game.

For a while my initial inspiration was drawn from [asciiart.eu](https://www.asciiart.eu/), I was thinking of taking ascii art of clouds from there, but I determined that it wasn't good enough. I thought of drawing ascii clouds but it turned out quite bad so I thought of a system in godoom to render sprites as ascii art.

I was creating sprites in [Aseprite](https://www.aseprite.org/) and while I was satisfied with the first pass of how godoom processes sprites, but I thought maybe I could have a some sort of ASCII fallback.

I looked into how create aseprite extensions, worked a couple of hours, and created this.

![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/egordunaev/aseprite-ascii/master/img/cool-logo.png)
[egordunaev/aseprite-ascii](https://github.com/egordunaev/aseprite-ascii)

Aseprite extension for creating ASCII art from current view. Currently supports output as raw text and new layer with aseprite default font. I had a couple of ideas for future features, but I'll see the general feedback first.
